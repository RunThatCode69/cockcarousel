Cock Carousel: Slide Rush

How do I play?

Tap the right side of the screen to jump, tap again in the air to flip, hold the left side to duck. Eat eggplants to grow and finish the level. Avoid everything else.

No sound?

Flip your ringer switch on, or turn the volume up — the game plays sound even on silent, but iOS sometimes needs one tap first.

I lost my progress

Progress is saved on your device. Deleting and reinstalling the app resets it.

Something's broken

Email bpoulter2019@gmail.com with your iPhone model and what happened. Screenshots help.
