Privacy Policy

Cock Carousel: Slide Rush · effective September 27, 2026

Cock Carousel does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. Full stop.

  • No accounts. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • No analytics, no ads, no trackers. The app contains no third-party SDKs.
  • No network use. The game runs entirely on your device and works offline.
  • Local save only. Your best score and highest level are stored on your device in the app's own storage. Deleting the app deletes them. They never leave your phone.

Because no data is collected, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If a future update ever changes this, this page and the App Store listing will be updated first.
