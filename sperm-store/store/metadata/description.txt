You are the least promising swimmer in the regiment, and you have just been dropped down the wrong tunnel.

Mission one: get out. Mission two: cross the entire map and beat two hundred million rivals to the finish line.

ONE THUMB
Hold to swim up. Let go to sink. That is the whole control scheme. Plays in portrait or landscape, with no account, no ads and nothing tracked.

A CAPTAIN WITH OPINIONS
Captain Ramrod talks you through every level with the total confidence of a man who has never once been right. Collect five hearts in a row and the whole level surges forward.

A PROPER BRIEFING
Typewriter title cards, a night drop, radio chatter and a reveal you will see coming about four seconds before your character does.

DIE INTERESTINGLY
Every wipeout comes with a fresh eulogy, and you are going to read a lot of them.

Rude, cartoonish and completely harmless. A military shooter parody for people who are bad at military shooters.
