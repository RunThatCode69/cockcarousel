Meet the happiest little guy on the internet. He has a face, he has arms, and he is about to go down the world's longest slide.

Your job is to keep him moving. Tap to jump, tap again for a backflip, hold to duck. Eat eggplants to grow. Dodge absolutely everything else.

TEN LEVELS
Candy Hills, Crab Beach, Bee Meadow, Cactus Canyon, Night Shift, Shrinkage Slopes, Hot Sauce Hollow, Thunder Thighs, Disco Inferno and The Big Finish. Each one has its own weather, its own hazards and its own terrible jokes. Clear them all to unlock Encore mode, where it only gets faster.

HAZARDS
Cacti. Mousetraps. Crabs, obviously. Bees you have to duck under. Shivering ice cubes. Furious chili peppers. Sometimes two at once.

MADE FOR ONE THUMB
Sixty-second runs, instant restarts, progress saved. Plays in landscape or portrait. No account, no ads, no in-app purchases, nothing tracked.

It is a cartoon. It is rude. Relax.
